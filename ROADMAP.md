# Roadmap

> Directional, not a promise. Community project, no SLA.

## dsh-os-suite 0.2.0

- trace: SQLite/OTel ingestion, signed evidence bundles.
- lease: real workspace writes + rollback journal.
- quota: enforcement hooks; eval: real runner/session replay.
- catalog: live registry adapter; registry: real health probes.

## Non-goals

- UI skins, generic memory, document pipelines, marketplaces.
- Claims of being official or audited.
- Publishing without provenance.
